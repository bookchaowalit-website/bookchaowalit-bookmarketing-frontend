# BookMarketing — Design Direction

BookMarketing is an editorial campaign desk built like a small printed issue:
warm paper, red registration ink, blue editorial emphasis, serif display type,
and channel tabs that read like a sequence of pages rather than a card grid.

The campaign file rail, promise line, channel sequence, and editorial move are
the product surface. Every campaign is synthetic and the interaction logs the
next publishable move locally; it does not claim live analytics or publishing
integrations.
