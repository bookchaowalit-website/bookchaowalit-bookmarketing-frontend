# Product: BookMarketing — Editorial Campaign Desk

## Product surface

BookMarketing is an illustrative editorial campaign desk for choosing a book launch angle, sequencing channel work, and keeping the next publishable move visible.

## Primary user

An independent author or operator turning one book idea into a focused, repeatable campaign.

## Product promise

Turn a book promise into a campaign sequence with a next publishable move, not a pile of channel metrics.

## Boundaries

- Use synthetic books, channels, and campaign signals only.
- Do not imply live analytics, publishing, or advertising integrations.
- The demo is a product design study, not a marketing automation service.

## Design mode

operate

## Interaction contract

- Selecting a campaign changes the story angle and channel sequence.
- Selecting a channel changes the focused editorial task.
- The next-move control marks one task ready locally and advances the focus.
